DYSKEI.

Six students. One discipline: technology. We are a Department of Technology & Society team at SUNY Korea, building a single window onto the visual rhetoric of technology on YouTube. Each member chose a different field of consumer technology to study under Dr. Anthony J. Pennings's Visual Rhetoric and Information Technology course.

Meet the team YouTube analyses

/// About this site

A group built around one question.

How does a YouTube channel about technology convince a viewer that its host is worth trusting? Each DYSKEI member picked a single technology creator and applied the EST 240 frameworks of film theory, semiotics, and Aristotelian rhetoric to read the channel's visual choices. The five channels below cover five very different kinds of technology, from NASA-grade engineering spectacle to drone hardware to consumer audio reviews. The group page assembles them as one set, so a reader can move between fields without losing the throughline that ties them together.

/// Five technology lenses

Five fields. Five creators. Five readings.

Each DYSKEI member chose a different corner of the technology landscape on YouTube. Below is a one-paragraph map of what each chosen channel covers and what its member's analysis is paying attention to.

D · Member 01 · Dohoon Lee

Mark Rober

Applied Engineering · STEM Spectacle

A former NASA engineer who turns mechanical design, physics, and CGI into theatrical engineering challenges. The channel's argument is that hard science is worth watching when staged as a story. Dohoon Lee reads it for how the production design makes engineering feel approachable without softening the technical specifics.

View analysis
Y · Member 02 · Youngjun Hong

The Drone Creative

Aerial Imaging · Camera Hardware

A specialist channel covering DJI Pocket gimbal cameras, drones, and other portable cinema hardware. Authority is built through hands-on field footage and frame-accurate side-by-sides. Youngjun Hong reads it as a study in how niche-hardware reviewers earn trust by letting the footage itself do the talking.

View analysis
S · Member 03 · Seongmin Park

Marques Brownlee · MKBHD

Consumer Electronics · Premium Reviews

The most cited consumer technology reviewer on YouTube. MKBHD has maintained a single visual identity across more than a decade of phones, audio, and EV coverage, and operates as the reference standard for trust engineering on the platform. Seongmin Park reads the Sony WH-1000XM6 review through that lens.

View analysis
K · Member 04 · Kyulee Kim

Allen Pan

DIY Electronics · Maker Engineering

A maker-engineer who builds physical projects that look absurd and operate as real high-voltage hardware. The channel routinely converts pop-culture artifacts into functional engineering, with humor and visible failure as part of the technique. Kyulee Kim reads it as applied electronics in disguise.

View analysis
E · Member 05 · Eric (Sunghun) Park

Mrwhosetheboss

Mobile Tech · Smartphone Reviews

Arun Maini's 22.5 million subscriber smartphone review channel, recognisable by its calm host persona and stained-glass church-window mise-en-scène. The channel argues that consumer tech is worth a cinematic frame. Eric Park reads it through Bordwell & Thompson, Chandler, and Aristotle in the Pocket Authority essay.
