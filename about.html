How the acronym was built

DYSKEI was not designed before the team. The team came first. Each of us contributed the first letter of our name in seating order, and once D, Y, S, K, E, and I were arranged on the page they spelled a word we did not invent. Read aloud, it lands close to the Korean phrase used to say I like you very much. The coincidence held meaning we hadn't planned, and we kept it.

Why the order is fixed

The letter order is preserved across every page on this site, from the nav bar's spelling to each member's role chip. Reading DYSKEI as a sequence rather than a set is part of the rhetorical move. The acronym only carries its second meaning if the letters appear in this exact order.
