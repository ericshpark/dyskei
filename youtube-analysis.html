/// YouTube Channel Analysis

Six channels. Six readings.

Each DYSKEI member analyzed a different YouTube creator for the EST 240 individual essay. Below is every analyzed video, in member order, alongside the link to that member's full essay.

D · Member 01 · Dohoon Lee · Applied Engineering

Mark Rober · I Engineered The Perfect First Date

The Mark Rober channel turns mechanical engineering and applied physics into spectacle-driven STEM content. Former NASA engineer, theatrical production values. Dohoon Lee reads the channel through the EST 240 frameworks to show how it makes hard engineering legible to a general audience.

Dohoon's full essay Open on YouTube

Y · Member 02 · Youngjun Hong · Aerial Imaging Hardware

The Drone Creative · DJI Pocket 4 vs Pocket 3

The Drone Creative reviews compact gimbal cameras, drone systems, and aerial-imaging hardware. Authority is built through hands-on field footage and frame-accurate side-by-sides. Youngjun Hong reads the channel as a study in how niche-hardware reviewers earn viewer trust through the footage itself.

Youngjun's full essay Open on YouTube

S · Member 03 · Seongmin Park · Consumer Electronics

Marques Brownlee / MKBHD · Sony WH-1000XM6 Review

MKBHD is the most cited consumer-technology reviewer on YouTube. A single visual identity has been held across more than a decade of phone, audio, and EV coverage, and the channel functions as the reference standard for trust engineering on the platform. Seongmin Park reads the audio review through that lens.

Seongmin's full essay Open on YouTube

K · Member 04 · Kyulee Kim · DIY Electronics

Allen Pan · 900V Capacitor Thor's Hammer

Allen Pan builds physical projects that look absurd and operate as real high-voltage hardware. The channel routinely turns pop-culture artifacts into working engineering, with humor and visible failure as part of the technique. Kyulee Kim reads it as applied electronics in disguise.

How to read these six together

The six channels are intentionally diverse, but a common thread runs through every analysis: each member treats the chosen channel as a system of visual choices, not as a stream of raw content. Lighting, framing, cutting rhythm, on-screen graphics, and host persona are read as the channel's argument for its own credibility. The DYSKEI group page does not duplicate the full essays. It assembles them as a single set, so the reader can move from one creator's grammar to the next without losing the throughline.
