/// Brand · Identity

The DYSKEI mark.

A 3D hexagonal emblem with interlocking angular frames, in royal blue metallic with silver chrome beveled edges. Below the mark, the DYSKEI wordmark is set in wide-spaced silver block letters with a subtle blue glow. Cinematic dark navy field, photorealistic render.

/// Primary mark

Hexagon. D. Chrome.

The hexagon carries the geometry of a circuit cell. The interlocking faces of the D resolve out of the hexagon's volume, beveled at the same angle on every edge. Royal blue carries the technology theme. Chrome silver carries the precision. The wordmark sits beneath, wide-spaced to feel architectural rather than typographic.

The AI prompt

Per the assignment requirement to disclose the prompt and the tool used. The prompt below produced the hero rendering shown above on the first pass, after the team agreed on the hexagonal direction.

/ DYSKEI logo prompt Make a logo for a team called "DYSKEI," formed from the initials of six members. The logo features a 3D hexagonal emblem with interlocking angular frames and a "D" shape at the center, in royal blue metallic with silver chrome beveled edges. Below, "DYSKEI" is written in wide-spaced silver 3D block letters with a subtle blue glow. Dark navy background, cinematic lighting, photorealistic 3D render style.

The acronym

DYSKEI was formed using the initials of our six members in their seating order: Dohoon Lee, Youngjun Hong, Seongmin Park, Kyulee Kim, Eric (Sunghun) Park, and Imran Mohamed. The resulting word was unintentional, yet when read aloud, it sounds remarkably close to the Japanese phrase for I like you very much (Daisuki). We kept the order and meaning exactly as they appeared.

Why the brand works at small sizes

The hexagonal silhouette holds up at small scales because the outer contour is symmetrical and the central D is the only interior shape. Stripped of color, the mark still reads as a hexagon with a recognizable initial. The favicon shipped with this site is a single SVG polygon plus a single letter, generated from the same geometry as the hero mark.
